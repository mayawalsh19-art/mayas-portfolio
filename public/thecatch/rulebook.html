The Catch is a dating card game with a phone for a matchmaker. The phone shows you who's on the market. The cards hold their secrets. Peek, bluff, date or ghost — then flip the cards, add them up, and find out who you really went home with.

1
Who's Playing
Pick who you're looking for (girls, guys or everyone), then add 2–6 players on the phone — a name and a player type each. The cards are the game; the phone just shows who's on the market and keeps score.
2
Pick a Player Type
Your type changes what every card is worth to you. Apply it yourself when you add up your cards.
💘 The Romantic — every green flag +1
🎯 The Selective — every negative −1 more
🔥 The Chaotic One — negatives +2 (max 0)
🌀 The Overthinker — −1 → −3, 0 → −1
🚪 The Avoidant — +3s −1, −2s & worse −1
💰 The Gold Digger — +2s & up +1, −2s & worse −1
3
Write Your Own (Optional)
Write real behaviors you've seen on the blank cards and shuffle them into the deck. They play like any other card — green blanks are +2, red blanks are −2.
4
Set the Table
Shuffle the deck and put it face-down in the middle. Give everyone 3 stalk chips. Press START — the phone lines up 7 profiles, and one of them is secretly a Catfish.

How a Match Works
Section 02
HOW A MATCH
WORKS
1
Meet the Profile
The phone shows who's on the market: name, age, archetype and an illustrated avatar.
2
Deal
Deal 6 cards off the top of the deck: 2 face-up, 4 face-down. Those six cards are this person.
3
Stalk
Spend a chip to secretly peek at one face-down card, then put it back. Everyone sees you stalk; nobody sees what you found.
4
Decide
On 3, everyone says DATE or GHOST out loud at the same time.
5
Flip, Add Up & Enter
Flip the face-down cards. If you dated, add up all 6 cards using your type and type your total into the phone. If you ghosted, tap GHOSTED. The phone scores the match and shows the leaderboard. Discard the six cards.
♥DATETable
Add up all 6 cards for your type. A negative total costs a heart. Always allowed.
◌GHOST3 each
Pass for ±0 — or +1 if it was the Catfish. When your 3 ghosts are gone, you have to date.
🔍STALK3 chips
Secretly peek at one face-down card. Stack chips on one profile if you like.
📱THE PHONECompanion
Shows the profile, applies the bonuses and penalties, keeps the leaderboard and hands out titles.

6
Your Type Changes the Math
The printed value is the base. Run every card through your type before you add up — a Romantic's +2 is a +3; an Overthinker's −1 is a −3. That total is what you enter.
7
Hearts
Everyone starts with 3. You lose one each time you DATE and your total is negative — and dating the Catfish always costs one. At 0 hearts you're Heartbroken: every positive score is halved.

🪝 The Catfish
One profile in matches 2–5 is secretly a Catfish. Nothing on the phone gives it away — it's revealed only after everyone has entered their scores. DATED it: −4 on top, plus a heart. GHOSTED it: +1.

Endgame & Titles
Section 04
ENDGAME &
TITLES
8
After Match 7
The highest total wins. Tied? Whoever kept more hearts wins. Still tied? You share it.
9
Everyone Gets a Title
The phone gives every player a title based only on their final score — the winner and everyone else.
Final titles
18 or more
The Catch — every call landed. Everyone at the table wants your number.
10 – 17
The Romantic — your heart led the way, and it mostly paid off.
1 – 9
The Situationship — some sparks, some red flags. It's complicated.
0 or less
The Red Flag Magnet — it wasn't you. (It was a little bit you.)
♛ Play again
Shuffle everything back together — discards and all — and press PLAY AGAIN. Seven new profiles, a new Catfish, and a chance to change your title.

Online Play
Section 06
PLAYING
ONLINE

Not in the same room? Online mode deals the trait cards digitally, on everyone's own phone. Same rules — the phones just do the dealing and the math.

1
Join a Room
Press ↗ PLAY ONLINE. One player taps CREATE A ROOM, picks who the room is looking for, and shares the 4-letter code; everyone else taps JOIN A ROOM. Each player picks a name and a type. You need at least 2 players.
2
Your Hand
Every phone is dealt the profile's 6 trait cards — 2 face-up, 4 face-down — drawn just like the printed deck. Tap a face-down card to stalk it: it spends a token and flips for you only.
3
Decide & Reveal
Everyone taps DATE or GHOST in secret. When the last player locks in, every card flips, the host's phone scores everyone for their type, and the leaderboard updates. The host moves to the next match.

Strategy & Tips
Section 07
STRATEGY
& TIPS
▲
Watch who stalks
A peek is secret, but the chip isn't. Someone stalked and then ghosted? They probably found a red flag.
▲
Bluff your peeks
Found a +3? Hesitate. Found a −3? Look pleased. Your face is a card too.
▲
Catfish detection
Anyone could be the Catfish, and the phone won't tell you until it's too late. In matches 2–5, a profile that looks too good is worth a stalk before you date it.
▲
Save ghosts for the worst
You only get 3, and when they're gone you have to date. Don't spend them on profiles that might be fine.
▲
Chase chemistry
A great date only earns the +2 if you're the only one who went. Sometimes the best move is the one nobody else makes.
▲
Protect your hearts
At 0 hearts your good dates only count half. Down to 1? Ghost the risky ones.
▲
Know your type's math
A yellow-flag-heavy profile is fine for a Chaotic One and a disaster for an Overthinker. Before you say DATE, count the cards your type changes.
The Golden Rule
Every profile has 6 cards. You see 2 before deciding. Everything else is a bet. The game isn't about being right — it's about making better bets than everyone else.
