The Catch is a dating game played with a phone and a deck of trait cards. The app shows you who's on the market. The cards hold their secrets. Peek, bluff, date or ghost — then flip the cards and find out who you really went home with.

How a Round Works
Section 02
HOW A ROUND
WORKS
1
Deal
The app shows the profile — name, archetype and an illustrated avatar — and the cards to deal. Put the first 2 face-up (the visible traits, always their mildest) and the other 4 face-down.
2
Look & Stalk
Before you decide, dig. 👁 LOOK in the app swaps the avatar for their real black-and-white photo. 🔍 STALK at the table: spend a chip, secretly peek at one face-down card, put it back. Tell no one.
3
Decide
Each player takes the phone in turn — "Pass the device" — and taps ♥ DATE or ◌ GHOST in private.
4
Flip & Score
Flip the face-down cards one by one as the app reveals them. The app scores everyone for their type and shows the leaderboard. Put the cards back in the deck — the app may deal them again later.
♥DATEApp
Score every card, adjusted by your type. A negative total costs a heart. Always allowed.
◌GHOSTApp · 3 each
Pass for ±0 — or +1 if it was the Catfish. When your 3 ghosts are gone, you have to date.
👁LOOKApp · 3 each
See their real photo — once per profile. You always see the avatar first; the photo only after a LOOK.
🔍STALKTable · 3 chips
Spend a chip to secretly peek at one face-down card. Stack them on one profile if you like.

5
Your Type Changes the Math
The printed value is the base. The app runs every card through your type before adding up — a Romantic's +2 is a +3; an Overthinker's −1 is a −3.
6
Hearts
Everyone starts with 3. You lose one each time you DATE and your total is negative — and dating the Catfish always costs one. At 0 hearts you're Heartbroken: every positive score is halved (the ½ in your bar) and you can't face The One.

🪝 The Catfish
One profile per game (rounds 2–5) is a Catfish. It looks like everyone else — a normal name, archetype and avatar — but its 2 face-up cards are green flags and all 4 face-down cards are −3. DATE it: −4 on top, plus a heart. GHOST it: +1 and Catfish Dodger.

The One, Endgame & Results
Section 04
THE ONE &
ENDGAME
💘 What Is "The One"?
After 7 rounds, everyone with 10+ points and at least 1 heart meets a final legendary profile — The One. There are no cards for this one: it plays out entirely in the app. One trait is visible (+2); four are hidden: 0, +2, −1 and a big +5 — +8 before your type gets its say.
7
Face The One
Each qualified player chooses ♛ TAKE A CHANCE or ◌ WALK AWAY in private. If your type-adjusted total is 7 or more, you found The One: +10. Under 7: −10 and a heart. Walking away changes nothing. Nobody qualified? Straight to results.
8
Results
Highest total wins. Everyone gets a title — The Catch, The Romantic, The Ghost, The Fun Ghoster, The Risk Taker or The Selective One — and their achievements. Playing solo, you're ranked against your 2 rivals.

Online Play
Section 06
PLAYING
ONLINE

Not in the same room? Online mode plays The Catch on everyone's own phone — no cards needed. The traits live on screen instead.

1
Join a Room
Press ↗ PLAY ONLINE. One player taps CREATE A ROOM and shares the 4-letter code; everyone else taps JOIN A ROOM. Each player picks a name and a type. You need at least 2 players.
2
Decide Together
Everyone sees the same profile and decides at the same time, in secret, with a 30-second timer — let it run out and you ghost. The host moves the game to the next round.
⚡STEALOnline · 1 use
Skip the date and take up to 3 points from the highest-scoring other player — never more than they have.
♥♥DOUBLE DATEOnline
If 2+ players pick it, the score is split evenly between them. Pick it alone and it's a normal date.

Strategy & Tips
Section 07
STRATEGY
& TIPS
▲
Watch who stalks
A peek is secret, but the chip isn't. Someone stalked and then ghosted? They probably found a red flag.
▲
Bluff your peeks
Found a +3? Hesitate. Found a −3? Look pleased. Your face is a card too.
▲
Catfish detection
Profiles usually lead with their mildest traits — two strong green flags face-up is suspicious. Spend a LOOK: the Catfish's photo looks a lot older than their age.
▲
Save ghosts for the worst
You only get 3, and when they're gone you have to date. Don't spend them on profiles that might be fine.
▲
Chase chemistry
At the table, a great date only earns the +2 if you're the only one who went. Sometimes the best move is the one nobody else makes.
▲
Protect your hearts
Hearts are your ticket to The One — and at 0 your good dates only count half. Down to 1? Ghost the risky ones.
▲
Know your type before The One
Every type clears The One's 7-point bar — except The Overthinker, whose math lands on 5. Overthinkers: walk away.
The Golden Rule
Every profile has 6 cards. You see 2 before deciding. Everything else is a bet. The game isn't about being right — it's about making better bets than everyone else.
