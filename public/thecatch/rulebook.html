Rulebook — How to Play
Players
2 – 6
Rounds
7 matches
Time
20 – 40 min
You need
The deck + 1 phone

The Catch is a dating card game with a phone for a matchmaker. The phone shows who's on the market. The cards hold their secrets. Peek, bluff, date or ghost, then flip the cards, add them up, and find out who you really went home with.

SET UP & PLAY
In the box
64 trait cards
Green, yellow and red flags with their point values
6 blank cards
3 green (+2) and 3 red (−2) to write your own
30 stalk chips
3 per player
One phone
bymayawalsh.design → The Catch → Play. No app, no login.
Setup
A
Who's playing
On the phone, pick who you're looking for (girls, guys or everyone), then add 2–6 players, each with a name and a player type (page 3).
B
Write your own (optional)
Write real behaviors on the blank cards and shuffle them in. Green blanks are +2, red blanks are −2.
C
Set the table
Shuffle the deck face-down in the middle. Give everyone 3 stalk chips. Press START THE GAME.
Each match
1
Meet them
The phone shows who's on the market: name, age and archetype.
2
Deal
Deal 6 cards: 2 face-up, 4 face-down. Those six cards are this person.
3
Stalk
Spend a chip to secretly peek at one face-down card, then put it back. Everyone sees you stalk; nobody sees what you found.
4
Decide
On 3, everyone says DATE or GHOST out loud. You have 3 ghosts; when they're gone, you have to date.
5
Flip, add up & enter
Flip every card. If you dated, add up all 6 using your type and enter your total on the phone. If you ghosted, tap Ghosted. Discard the six cards.

Tips
1
Watch who stalks
The peek is secret, but the chip isn't. Someone stalked and then ghosted? They probably found a red flag.
2
Bluff your peeks
Found a +3? Hesitate. Found a −3? Look pleased. Your face is a card too.
3
Save ghosts for the worst
You only get 3. Don't spend them on profiles that might be fine.
4
Chase chemistry
A great date only earns +2 if you're the only one who went.
5
Know your type's math
Before you say Date, count the cards your type changes.
6
Add up fast
Add the printed values, then fix only the cards your type changes. Say your total out loud before you enter it.
Play again
Shuffle everything back together and press PLAY AGAIN: seven new profiles, a new catfish, and a chance to change your title.
