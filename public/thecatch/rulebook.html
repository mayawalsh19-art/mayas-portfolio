Setup & Getting Started
Section 01
SETUP &
HOW TO START
1
Open the Game
Visit bymayawalsh.design, tap the The Catch card, then press ▶ PLAY THE GAME. Runs entirely in-browser — no app, no login required.
2
Choose a Mode
PLAY SOLO — you vs. AI opponents on one device. PLAY ONLINE — everyone joins from their own phone using a 4-letter room code (like Jackbox). Host creates the room, others join.
3
Enter Your Name
Pick a name and an avatar emoji. Your name appears on the leaderboard and in score callouts. In online mode, everyone sets this up on their own device before the host starts.
4
Pick a Player Type
Each type changes how trait values score for you. Same profile, completely different results. Locked in for the whole game.
💘 The Romantic — positives +1
🎯 The Selective — negatives −1 more
🔥 The Chaotic One — negatives +2
🌀 The Overthinker — −1 traits → −3
🚪 The Avoidant — extremes drop −1
💰 The Gold Digger — strong positives +1
5
Add Custom Traits or People (Optional)
Before starting, add real-life traits you've seen (toggle green or red) or build a full custom person with a name and their actual traits. Both get mixed into the profiles everyone will date.
6
Press START
The game generates 7 unique profiles — including a randomly placed Catfish. No two games play the same. Every round a new profile is revealed and the decision phase begins.

How a Round Works
Section 02
HOW A ROUND
WORKS

Each round introduces one profile. You see some traits immediately — others are hidden. You decide whether to date them before the full picture is revealed. 7 rounds, then a final boss.

1
Read the Profile
Each profile shows their name and 2 visible traits — usually the more moderate flags. Four additional traits are hidden behind question marks and revealed after everyone decides.
2
Choose Your Action
Before the hidden traits are revealed, every player locks in one of the following actions. In online multiplayer, everyone decides simultaneously on their own device.
♥ DATE Core
Score all of the profile's traits. Positive total = points. Negative total = lose a heart. Your player type adjusts all values.
◌ GHOST Core
Skip the round. No points gained or lost. You have 3 ghosts per game — once gone, you must date or use another action.
🔍 STALK Intel
Reveal one hidden trait before deciding. Costs one stalk token (3 per game). Then choose DATE or GHOST with better info.
⚡ STEAL Online
Take 3 points from the current score leader. One use per game. If you're the leader, steal from second place.
♥♥ DOUBLE DATE Online
Both players date together and split the score. Lower risk, lower reward. Only activates if 2+ players both choose it.
🛋️ THERAPY Recovery
Auto-skips your next round, then rolls 50/50 for +4 or +0. One use per player. Best used after a string of bad dates.

Scoring & Traits
Section 03
SCORING &
TRAIT SYSTEM
3
Traits Reveal One by One
After everyone locks in their decision, all hidden traits reveal automatically — one per second. Each trait flashes its value as it appears.
Trait Value Scale
Flag Type Base Value Color
Green Flag — positive traits, good vibes +1 to +3 ● Mint
Yellow Flag — neutral, mildly concerning 0 to −1 ● Gold
Red Flag — negative traits, actual problems −2 to −3 ● Neon Red
4
Your Player Type Adjusts Every Score
All trait values are modified by your player type before totaling. The Romantic adds +1 to every positive. The Selective deducts 1 from every negative. Same profile, completely different results — this is the whole game.
5
Score Is Announced
After all traits reveal, a score popup shows your round result. Positive scores glow mint, negative glow red. Your running total updates on the leaderboard.
6
Hearts Track Your Risky Dates
Start with 3 hearts. Every time you DATE and your round total is negative, you lose one heart. Losing all 3 doesn't eliminate you — but makes you ineligible for The One. Hearts are only lost on a negative DATE, never for ghosting, stalking, stealing, or therapy.
🪝 The Catfish — Special Rule
One profile every game is a Catfish. Visible traits look perfect, but all hidden traits are −3. DATE them: take a −4 penalty on top. GHOST them: earn +1 and "Catfish Dodger." No label tells you who it is — trust your gut.

The One, Endgame & Results
Section 04
THE ONE &
ENDGAME
💘 What Is "The One"?
After 7 rounds, players with ≥ 10 points and at least 1 heart face a final legendary profile — The One. Their one hidden trait is worth +5 and can flip the entire leaderboard. If you score negative, you lose your final heart and the points.
7
Face The One (if qualified)
10+ points and 1+ heart → you enter The One screen. One choice: DATE (risk it for +5) or GHOST (safe exit, no change). Score negative and you lose your last heart plus the points.
8
Tiebreaker (if needed)
If two or more players end with the same score, a Tiebreaker activates — one final profile, all tied players decide simultaneously. Fastest to resolve wins.
9
Results & Achievements
Final leaderboard shows all players ranked by points. Achievements earned appear as emoji badges below each score — the story of how you played, not just the number.

Strategy & Tips
Section 06
STRATEGY
& TIPS
▲
Save ghosts for the worst profiles
You only get 3. Don't waste them early on profiles that might be fine — ghost the ones where the visible traits are already suspicious and you have no stalk tokens left.
▲
Stalk before committing to a date
Revealing even one hidden trait can change the math dramatically. If the visible traits look middling, spend a stalk token before deciding. +3 and −3 traits feel completely different when revealed.
▲
Catfish detection
The Catfish always looks perfect on the surface. If every visible trait is a green flag with no yellows or reds, that's suspicious. Stalk one hidden trait — if it's deeply negative, ghost immediately.
▲
Use Steal at the right moment
Stealing 3 points is most effective in round 5 or 6. Too early and the leader changes. Too late and the gap may be too large. Use it when you're within striking distance.
▲
Therapy is an investment
The 50/50 on +4 or +0 is only worth it when you need points and can afford to skip a round. Don't use it round 1 — use it after brutal red flag dates in rounds 4–6.
▲
Protect your hearts for The One
Hearts are your ticket to the final round. If you're above 10 points but lost all 3 hearts, you can't face The One. Ghost aggressively in the final 2 rounds if you're sitting on 1 heart.
▲
Coordinate Double Dates
Double Date only works if 2+ players choose it simultaneously — without communicating. If you suspect someone will date the same profile, choosing Double Date cuts your risk. If they ghost, you date normally.
The Golden Rule
Every profile has 6 traits. You only see 2 before deciding. Everything else is a bet. The game isn't about being right — it's about making better bets than everyone else.
