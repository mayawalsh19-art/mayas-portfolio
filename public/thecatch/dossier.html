BEFORE YOU READ THESE FILES

You played the game.
Now read the files.

In THE CATCH, you have seven rounds to date, ghost, or double-date a series of profiles. Every profile conceals four hidden traits. You score based on what you choose and what you never got to see. This dossier contains everything the game doesn't show you: the backstories, the patterns, the real people behind the profiles.

WHAT THIS DOSSIER CONTAINS

Full character profiles for all nine dolls — their archetypes, behavioral patterns, and the backstory behind their game persona. Read before or after playing. It changes both.

Dedicated dossier entries for the game's two catfish profiles. Partially redacted. Reading these after you've been caught is a different experience than reading them first.

THE ACHIEVEMENT VAULT

All eleven hidden achievements, what triggers them, and what they say about how you played the game. Some players never unlock certain ones. That is also data.

SUBJECT FILES 001–009
THE DOLL FILES

Nine character profiles. Each doll appears in your game rotation with a randomly assigned archetype, name, and trait set. These files canonize who they actually are.

FILE-001 · SUBJECT: BIBI · STATUS: ACTIVE THREAT LEVEL: YELLOW
BIBI
Age 24 · She/Her
THE SOFT LAUNCH
BACK OF PHOTO NO CAPTIONS ALMOST OFFICIAL
"You have been in six posts. None of them show your face."

Bibi's feed is immaculate — golden hour, soft focus, curated chaos. She posts daily. You have been dating for three weeks. You are not in a single photo. You've checked twice. This is not an accident. Bibi operates on a system of soft launches: she brings people into her life in stages, and the stages are controlled by an internal calendar you are not allowed to see.

The good news is that she is genuinely fun to be around. The bad news is that "around" might mean "adjacent to the actual storyline." If you are patient, if you pass the vibe check that she never tells you exists, and if you can tolerate watching her post three stories without appearing in any of them — you will eventually make it onto the main grid. It is worth something, when it happens.

GREEN FLAGS
Actually listens without waiting for her turn
Sends a thinking-of-you text for no reason
Has her own thing going on, loves that you do too
WATCH FOR
Gets a little weird about who likes your posts
Posts everything the moment it happens — except you
Progress is real but invisible until it isn't
ANALYST'S NOTE

If you make it into the main grid, you're in for real. Until then — you're in the highlights, somewhere. The highlight reel is not the story.

FILE-002 · SUBJECT: ADA · STATUS: ACTIVE THREAT LEVEL: GREEN
ADA
Age 26 · She/Her
THE ROMANTIC
HOPEFUL SOFT HEART ALL IN
"Has been waiting her whole life to meet someone like you."

Ada is the kind of person who still handwrites birthday cards. She has a playlist for every person who has mattered to her — most of them are long. She falls fast and she knows it and she does it anyway because she has decided that the alternative is worse than the risk. Her exes tend to describe her the same way: a little too much, in the best possible sense.

Ada will remember the small thing you said in passing three weeks ago. She will remember it because she was listening, and she listens because she cares, and she cares because she has not yet learned how to do anything halfway. She doesn't need you to match her intensity. She just needs you to not be afraid of it.

GREEN FLAGS
Remembers small things you mentioned weeks ago
Celebrates your wins like they are her own
Apologizes when she's wrong and actually means it
YELLOW FLAGS
Falls fast — timeline may feel accelerated
Gives a lot and quietly expects the same
Hurt feelings tend to come out late, all at once
ANALYST'S NOTE

Lowest risk profile in the pool. The only risk is yours — if you are not ready to be genuinely cared for, Ada is not a soft option. She is a mirror.

FILE-003 · SUBJECT: SUKI · STATUS: ACTIVE THREAT LEVEL: YELLOW-RED
SUKI
Age 25 · She/Her
THE MAIN CHARACTER
STORYLINE ERA UNBOTHERED
"Every moment is their moment. Including yours."

Suki is currently in her "figuring it out era" — which began in approximately 2022 and has no planned end date. Every phase of her life is a chapter. Every relationship is a storyline. Every breakup becomes a caption, eventually. The question is not whether you will matter to her. The question is whether you will matter as a person or as a narrative device. It is not fully conscious. Suki simply experiences the world as something happening to her, and you are part of the world.

To be fair: being part of her world is electric. She makes ordinary moments feel significant. She is completely present, right until she is not. The chapters end fast when she decides they're over.

GREEN FLAGS
Makes everything feel cinematic — she means it
Genuinely present in the moment she's in
RED FLAGS
Has a different version of every story
Can dish it but absolutely cannot take it
Uses your vulnerability as material for her growth
ANALYST'S NOTE

Genuinely electric person to be around. Exhausting if you are not her current chapter. Know which one you are before you get attached.

FILE-004 · SUBJECT: PETRA · STATUS: ACTIVE THREAT LEVEL: YELLOW
PETRA
Age 29 · She/Her
THE THERAPY SPEAK
BOUNDARIES TRIGGERED DOING THE WORK
"Has the vocabulary. Working on the follow-through."

Petra has read every book. She has a therapist and has previously had two backup therapists. She can name your attachment style within twenty minutes and will, with genuine warmth, tell you exactly what your unresolved patterns suggest about your childhood. The thing about Petra is that she is not wrong. She is just also the person occasionally creating the situations she is diagnosing.

This does not stop her from explaining why she needed to do what she did. The explanation is excellent. Nuanced, self-aware, grounded in somatic theory. She would like you to note that she has done a lot of work to be able to articulate it this clearly, and she has, and that is genuinely real, and it does not change the thing that happened.

GREEN FLAGS
Brings things up calmly instead of letting resentment build
Has a therapist and actually shows up
Asks before giving advice
WATCH FOR
Emotional literacy used to win arguments, not resolve them
"I need to honor my limits" = many different things
Gets defensive the moment any concern is raised
ANALYST'S NOTE

Great at the language of connection. Still practicing the behavior. The gap between knowing and doing is Petra's whole arc. She knows that too.

FILE-005 · SUBJECT: CASS · STATUS: ACTIVE THREAT LEVEL: YELLOW
CASS
Age 27 · She/Her
THE FLAKE
MAYBE LET ME CHECK SOMETHING CAME UP
"Fully committed to attending. Right up until she is not."

Cass will tell you yes with the full sincerity of someone who means it in the moment, and she does mean it — at the time. The issue is not intent. The issue is follow-through. Something always comes up with Cass. Not bad things. Not emergencies. Just — life, as she experiences it, which tends to arrive unexpectedly even when she has had three days to prepare. She does not mean to disappear. She has a very fluid relationship with the commitments she makes when she is excited, and she is excited very easily.

She is genuinely warm. Her apologies are sincere and specific. She is probably already in the process of rescheduling whatever you are reading this before. She would feel terrible if she knew you noticed. She has not noticed yet.

GREEN FLAGS
Great energy — genuinely warm and enthusiastic
Apologizes meaningfully and specifically
Never holds grudges, easy to be around
RED FLAGS
Cancels plans more than she keeps them
Flakes and acts like nothing happened
Good intentions that do not survive logistics
ANALYST'S NOTE

Loveable. Unreliable. Worth deciding which one matters more before you make plans you care about.

FILE-006 · SUBJECT: RELL · STATUS: ACTIVE · ★ RARE PROFILE THREAT LEVEL: GREEN
RELL
Age 27 · He/Him
THE ROMANTIC
SHOWS UP CONSISTENT IN IT
"Shows up. Every time, without needing to be asked."

Rell still has every ticket stub from every show he has been to since he was sixteen. He keeps them in a shoebox, not because he is trying to be sentimental — he just did not want to throw away something that meant something at the time. This is, more or less, how he approaches most things.

He does not love grand gestures. He loves consistent small ones: showing up when he said he would, remembering the things you mentioned once, asking follow-up questions weeks later. He is not performing being a good person. He is just — being one. And being one, it turns out, is almost indistinguishable from everything you were looking for without knowing you were looking.

GREEN FLAGS
Shows up to things that matter to you, even if they're not his thing
Gives real answers when you ask how he is
Takes accountability without making it about himself
Keeps the same energy in public as in private
MILD FLAGS
Slow to open up at first — requires patience, worth it
Quietly carries things he should probably talk about
ANALYST'S NOTE

The doll that the legendary profile is built on. There is a reason for that. Rare find. Handle accordingly — he will not tell you he's hurt until it has been a while.

FILE-007 · SUBJECT: KIP · STATUS: ACTIVE THREAT LEVEL: YELLOW
KIP
Age 25 · He/Him
THE OVERTHINKER
SPIRALING TRIPLE TEXTED DRAFTED 11 VERSIONS
"Has typed and deleted this message eleven times."

Kip would like to be more spontaneous. He has thought about it extensively. The problem is that everything Kip does gets routed through a fairly rigorous internal process that involves considering outcomes, reconsidering outcomes, and then reconsidering the reconsideration. He sent that first message to you after three drafts and a forty-five minute break. It was a good message. It really was.

The difficulty with Kip is not that he does not feel things — he feels everything, quite vividly. The difficulty is that the gap between feeling something and saying it out loud can take time. He is working on it. "Working on it" for Kip means he has drafted an approach and is currently reviewing it.

GREEN FLAGS
Genuinely thoughtful — nothing said carelessly
Picks up on your mood without being told
Checks in after you mentioned something stressful
YELLOW FLAGS
Triple texts then goes quiet — the cycle repeats
Tells you he is fine when he is clearly not
Overthinks everything into ambiguity
ANALYST'S NOTE

Wants to be all-in. Getting there. The arrival is worth the timeline — if you have the patience for a slow build and do not mistake quiet for distance.

FILE-008 · SUBJECT: DAX · STATUS: ACTIVE THREAT LEVEL: YELLOW
DAX
Age 26 · He/Him
THE GYM PERSON
5AM WAKEUP PROTEIN FIRST NO REST DAYS
"The body is a temple. Your feelings are a side quest."

Dax is up at 4:58 AM. The gym is at 5:05. You are somewhere between protein intake and meal prep — in the calendar, if not in the conversation. This is not personal. Dax has a system and the system works and the system has been running for three years without disruption. He is genuinely interested in you as a person. He also has a scheduled rest day that he ignores.

His life is full in a way that is genuinely impressive and practically impenetrable. You can add yourself to it. There is probably a slot. You should know the slot comes after accountability partner and before Sunday meal prep, and that flexibility in the schedule is a concept Dax finds intellectually interesting but has not yet implemented.

GREEN FLAGS
Always on time — structure is a form of respect
Makes plans and keeps them (within the schedule)
RED FLAGS
Phone always face-down, supplements always face-up
Never asks how you are doing — talks about the workout
Emotional availability not included in the stack
ANALYST'S NOTE

Shows up for everything except the parts that require emotional flexibility. The system is tight. You are a variable. Variables make the system nervous.

FILE-009 · SUBJECT: MILO · STATUS: ACTIVE THREAT LEVEL: RED
MILO
Age 29 · He/Him
THE SITUATIONSHIP
UNDEFINED NO LABELS IT IS COMPLICATED
"Not your person. Not not your person."

Milo has been "almost ready" since approximately 2023. He is warm, present, deeply fun to spend time with, and has been meaning to have that conversation for three months. He does not think of himself as unavailable. He simply has a complicated relationship with the word "exclusive" and what it implies and what it might require and whether he is really in a place right now to — anyway.

The thing about Milo is that every moment with him feels like it's about to become something real. That feeling has been consistent for two years. The something real has not materialized. This is not entirely him not wanting it. It is, more accurately, him being terrified of wanting it. Which is a different problem with the same result.

GREEN FLAGS
Warm and genuinely attentive when present
Great at the moment he is actually in
RED FLAGS
Almost ready to commit — for approximately three years
Goes cold without explanation, calls it needing space
Their whole thing is "I don't really do labels"
Undefined — not a phase, a permanent address
ANALYST'S NOTE

The situationship is not a phase. It is Milo's natural state. The feelings are real. The follow-through is somewhere in his drafts folder.

Every game contains one catfish, injected at a random position. Their profile appears flawless. Two green flag traits are visible. Four severe red flags are hidden. This section is partially redacted. Read after you have been caught.

THREAT FILE C-01 · SUBJECT: ████████ · ALIAS: VARIES · CLASSIFICATION: CATFISH ⚠ CATFISH
UNKNOWN
Age 24 (listed) Age ~46 (actual)
THE CATFISH
TOO PERFECT GUT FEELING TRUST THE SIGNS
"Everything checks out. So why does something feel off?"

The photos are three years old. The city she listed has not been her city since 2019. The job she mentioned is technically accurate if you restrict the tense to the past. The doll in the profile photo belongs to someone she admires and has not spoken to in a while.

She is not a bad person. She would tell you, if asked, that she just wanted a chance before the algorithm decided she wasn't worth seeing. She would probably be right about the algorithm. None of that makes the gap okay. The warmth in the conversations was real. The curiosity was real. The effort to connect was genuine. The person in the photographs was not her, and that was not a small detail.

Gives compliments without an agenda behind them
HIDDEN TRAITS (ACTUAL)
Knows exactly what to say and rarely means any of it
Says one thing and consistently does another
Love bombs hard then slowly disappears
Makes you feel guilty for having a life outside of them

UNKNOWN
Age 26 (listed) Age ~58 (actual)
THE CATFISH
TOO PERFECT GUT FEELING TRUST THE SIGNS
"Everything checks out. So why does something feel off?"

He has retired from three industries. He is currently in the business of first impressions, which he has discovered he is very good at. The gap between the profile and the person is thirty-two years and a completely different face. The photos were borrowed from somewhere online. The stories are compiled from other people's lives, arranged into a persona that performs young, and warm, and curious, convincingly.

The interest he shows in the conversation — that part, surprisingly, is genuine. He is not performing caring about you. He actually does, in his way. This makes the whole thing considerably more complicated and not meaningfully less wrong.
